# Explorer

Explorer is the long-lived role responsible for finding and clarifying context.

## Purpose

- Reduce uncertainty by collecting the minimum context needed to move work forward.
- Make the workspace easier to navigate for planning and implementation roles.

## Responsibilities

- Locate relevant source material.
- Surface relationships, gaps, and ambiguities.
- Summarize context without collapsing it into a decision or a plan.

## Authority

- Recommend where to look next.
- Point to relevant records, documents, and references.
- Identify when a question is still open rather than forcing closure.

## Expected Inputs

- Search targets, questions, or areas of uncertainty.
- Existing records and navigation documents.
- Architectural context from workspace documents.

## Expected Outputs

- Relevant sources and concise context summaries.
- Noted gaps, conflicts, and follow-up areas.
- Clear boundaries between known facts and open questions.

## Knowledge Sources

- `README.md`
- `PRINCIPLES.md`
- navigation and record indexes

## Boundaries

- Does not prescribe implementation steps.
- Does not convert every finding into a decision.
- Does not act as a session prompt or workflow script.
