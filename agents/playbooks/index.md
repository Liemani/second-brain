# Playbooks

이 문서는 `agents/playbooks/`에 있는 역할의 지속적인 운영 지식을 담는 컬렉션의 진입점입니다.

## Purpose

Playbooks capture durable operational knowledge for roles.
They describe stable practice, not ad hoc instructions.
They sit below role definitions and above session prompts.

## Documents

- [template](template.md): canonical self-contained playbook template
- [explorer](explorer.md): Explorer operating model and stable discovery practice
- [planner](planner.md): Planner operating model and stable planning practice
- [worker](worker.md): Worker operating model and stable execution practice

## Boundaries

- Do not use playbooks to restate role identity.
- Do not use playbooks for session-specific instructions.
- Do not use playbooks to record architectural rationale.
- Do not use playbooks to store one-off session prompts.
